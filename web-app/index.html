TRIBUTE FROM THE FAMILY "Her children arise and call her blessed." Proverbs 31:28 To Our Beloved Auntie Bee, It is with hearts full of sorrow, yet with gratitude to God for a life well lived, that we pay this tribute to our beloved mother, grandmother, great-grandmother, sister, aunt, and matriarch of our family. Auntie Bee was more than a family member—she was the heart of our home. She was a woman of unwavering faith, quiet strength, and unconditional love. Her life was a testimony of sacrifice, resilience, kindness, and generosity. She welcomed everyone with open arms, offered wise counsel without judgment, and gave selflessly to all who came her way. To us, she was our comfort in difficult times, our voice of wisdom when we needed direction, and our source of encouragement whenever life became challenging. Her home was always a place of warmth, peace, and belonging, where every visitor was received with love and every family member knew they had someone who truly cared. Through the many seasons of her life, she remained steadfast in her faith and devoted to her family. She celebrated our joys, stood beside us in our trials, and taught us, by her own example, the values of humility, compassion, respect, forgiveness, and hard work. The legacy she leaves behind cannot be measured by material possessions, but by the countless lives she touched and the love she freely shared. Although her physical presence is no longer with us, the lessons she taught, the memories we created together, and the love she planted in our hearts will remain with us forever. We are deeply grateful for the privilege of calling her our own. As we mourn her passing, we also celebrate a life that was truly well lived. We take comfort in God's promise that those who trust in Him shall have everlasting life, and we pray that He grants her eternal rest and perfect peace. Dear Auntie Bee, thank you for your sacrifices, your prayers, your wisdom, and your unfailing love. Your place in our family can never be replaced, but your legacy will continue through every generation that follows. Though we will miss you beyond words, we rejoice in the assurance that you are now resting in the loving arms of your Creator. Rest peacefully, Auntie Bee. You will forever be loved, deeply missed, and never forgotten. Your memory will remain a guiding light in our family for generations to come. With love and gratitude.
The Family of Madam Agnes Pomaa

TRIBUTE BY THE CHURCH OF PENTECOST FOR THE LATE AGNES POMAA JOHN 14:2-4 The late Agnes Pomaa was baptized by immersion at the Church of Pentecost, Suhum Assembly, on the 20th day of June, 1966. She was baptized by Rev. Owusu, the then officiating minister. Mad. Agnes Pomaa came to Sankubenase together with her husband due to retirement in the year 1982. She offered her maximum support in establishing the local church by helping to win more souls to Christ. Mad. Agnes Pomaa served in the church as the treasurer for almost 15 years. She engaged in various activities of the church such as service to pastors and elders of the church, participating in all women's ministry activities, cleaning church premises, advising young women in the church and many more. Indeed she was a backbone and strong pillar of the local church, an active member for about 60 years. During her critical moments, the church showed her all the support she needed — sharing communion and the word of God with her, praying with her, regular visitations, and offering both financial and material support. But the Lord knows best; we wish our beloved a safe journey back to her Maker. ENA PA NANTE YIE 1 THESSALONIANS 4:14
The Church Of Pentecost, Central Assembly – Sankubenase
